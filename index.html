TempDiary

Live Product
tempdiary.co.uk Commercial Kitchen Food Hygiene & SFBB Diary

Ditch the greasy blue FSA binder. TempDiary is the dead-simple mobile temperature diary built for UK bakeries, cafes, restaurants, and dark kitchens. Takes staff under 30 seconds every morning.

FSA & EHO Compliance: Satisfies mandatory HACCP cold-holding checks (0°C–5°C target buffer, 8.0°C legal limit) under the Food Safety and Hygiene (England) Regulations.
  • Sign Opening & Closing Checks: 1-tap staff timestamp attribution to prove live morning compliance without retrospective guessing.
  • Mandatory Corrective Action Prompts: If a fridge runs warm (>8°C), the system flags alerts and logs remediation on the spot.
  • 1-Click EHO Inspection Packs: Instantly compile and email formatted 30-day SFBB PDF reports when the local council inspector walks in.
  • Unlimited Kitchen Scale: Unlimited fridges, freezers, sandwich counters, and staff devices with encrypted 6-month FSA cloud retention.

TriggerTrack

Live Product
triggertrack.co.uk Statutory HAVS & Tool Vibration Compliance

Ditch paper site clipboards. TriggerTrack calculates statutory Health and Safety Executive (HSE) vibration exposure points in real time on site, flags dangerous overruns, and protects against personal injury claims.

Control of Vibration at Work Regulations 2005: Real-time tracking against the 100 pt EAV (Exposure Action Value) and 400 pt ELV (Exposure Limit Value).
  • Pre-Loaded UK Fleet Catalog: Pre-calibrated m/s² vibration ratings for Stihl cut-off saws, Hilti breakers, JCB hydraulic units, and Wacker plates.
  • Instant Live HSE Point Calculation: Site supervisors select the operative, pick the tool, and slide runtime for automatic point totals.
  • Statutory EAV/ELV Overrun Alerts: Warns when an operative nears 100 points and enforces mandatory cessation before hitting 400 points.
  • HSE & Occupational Health Audit PDFs: 1-click exposure ledgers for HSE inspectors, occupational health surveillance, and insurers.
